Foreword for Joanne

Executive Note

Joanne, this was built to create a sellable asset for Joanne Gore Communications — something shaped around your definition of JGC, your language, your market, and the way you already see the work. The goal is to show a model that could be refined and moved toward market as quickly as you feel makes sense.

The idea behind METS is simple: take the strategic intelligence that currently lives inside JGC and organize it into modular operating playbooks that companies can use, license, learn from, and build around. The platform is only the delivery wrapper. The real value is the methodology.

This prototype uses Packaging + Labels as the first example because it sits naturally inside JGC’s print-industry roots. It is not presented as a final answer. It is a working model built so you can react to something real, shape it, reject parts of it, improve it, or decide whether it deserves to move forward at all.

The goal is not to replace JGC’s consulting work. It is to create a line of business where JGC can continue offering its strategic advisory and agency services while building scalable intellectual property that supports recurring revenue, client enablement, and future growth.

Just as importantly, this was designed with a bigger personal vision in mind for you: creating freedom for you to pursue the visibility opportunities that already fit who you are — speaking, lecturing, teaching, thought leadership, and broader industry influence — while the methodology you built continues creating value.
